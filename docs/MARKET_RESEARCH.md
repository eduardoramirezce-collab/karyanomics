# Karyanomics — Market Research & Sources

Every figure used in [`BUSINESS_PLAN.md`](../BUSINESS_PLAN.md) and
[`PITCH.md`](../PITCH.md), with its source. Compiled 2026-10-24. No figure is
invented; each links to a real, working source.

> **Caution:** market-size estimates differ widely between research firms
> because "pharmacogenomics" is defined differently (narrow test reagents vs.
> broad precision-medicine services). Cite a **range with the base year and
> source**, not a single headline number.
