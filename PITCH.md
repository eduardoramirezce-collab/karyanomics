# Karyanomics — Pitch

*A 10-slide deck outline. Every number is cited in
[`docs/MARKET_RESEARCH.md`](docs/MARKET_RESEARCH.md).*

---

### Slide 1 — Hook
The same pill helps you, fails your friend, and harms your mom.
**Genes are part of why.**

### Slide 2 — The pain
Each year in the U.S., adverse drug reactions cause:
- **1.5M** ER visits
- **~175,000** deaths
- **$30B+** in cost
*(CDC · FDA FAERS · NIH)*

### Slide 3 — It's everyone
- **90%+** of people carry a gene change that affects a drug.
- **~45%** of patients already take a drug with gene-based guidance.
*(Cell Reports Medicine 2023 · MDPI Pharmacy 2025)*

### Slide 4 — It works, and it's cheap
- **PREPARE trial: ~30% fewer** serious drug reactions with gene-guided care.
- Pays for itself at **~$114 per patient.**
*(The Lancet 2023 · Nature 2018)*

### Slide 5 — The product
Load your genes → pick a drug → get a plain answer in three levels:
- ✅ Typical response expected
- ⚠️ Your genes may change how this works
- 🛑 Talk to your prescriber first

Clinicians unlock full detail behind an NPI gate.
**Your genes never leave your device.**

### Slide 6 — The proof pair (live demo)
Our working demo: **clopidogrel** (a blood thinner). People whose genes clear it
poorly face **~3.5× the stent-clot risk.** *(NIH meta-analysis)*

### Slide 7 — The danger list (why it matters)
| Gene + drug | What happens |
|---|---|
| CYP2C19 + clopidogrel | 3.5× clot risk |
| HLA-B*57:01 + abacavir | screening → 0% severe reaction |
| HLA-B*15:02 + carbamazepine | zero severe skin reactions after screening |
| TPMT/NUDT15 + thiopurines | up to 20× toxicity |
| DPYD + 5-FU chemo | ~25× risk of treatment death |

### Slide 8 — The market
- **~$7.6B** today, growing **~8%/yr.**
- **70%+** of people say they would pay.
*(Grand View Research · MDPI/Frontiers)*

### Slide 9 — Why us
Rivals sell a **$300–$600 lab test.** We sell **the answer** from a file you
already own. **Built for all ancestries** — 78% of gene research skewed
European, and we serve the rest. *(Cell 2019)*

### Slide 10 — The ask
A **live demo you can click today.** Next: more drugs, the clinician portal,
real clinic pilots.
